GrowthOS 
Autonomous AI Growth & Agentic Commerce Platform

GrowthOS is an AI-powered growth engine designed to help businesses identify customer purchase opportunities, make intelligent growth decisions, execute personalized actions, and continuously learn from real business outcomes.

Instead of simply analyzing customer data, GrowthOS creates a closed-loop growth system:

Customer Behavior → Intent Detection → Opportunity → AI Decision → Action → Outcome → Learning**

## ✨ Key Features

- 🎯 **Customer Intent Detection**
  - Analyzes browsing and cart behavior.
  - Identifies high, medium, and low-intent customers.
  - Generates actionable growth opportunities.

- 🤖 **AI Growth Decisions**
  - Selects appropriate strategies based on customer intent.
  - Uses historical performance to improve decisions.
  - Calculates decision confidence.

- ⚡ **Autonomous Growth Actions**
  - Generates personalized customer actions.
  - Supports strategies such as cart recovery and product recommendations.
  - Tracks action lifecycle: Ready → Executing → Completed / Failed.

- 📊 **Outcome Analytics**
  - Records conversions and generated revenue.
  - Calculates conversion rates.
  - Measures average revenue per conversion.
  - Provides performance by action type.

- 🧠 **Strategy Learning**
  - Learns from historical action outcomes.
  - Compares strategy performance.
  - Identifies the best-performing growth strategies.
  - Uses historical conversion rates and revenue to influence future decisions.

- 📈 **Growth Dashboard**
  - Opportunities overview
  - Growth action pipeline
  - Conversion metrics
  - Revenue generated
  - Customer opportunities
  - Strategy performance
